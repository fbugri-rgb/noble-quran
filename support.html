Support

Answers to what comes up most, and a way to reach someone if none of them fit.

The recitation stops when I lock the screen

It shouldn't — playback continues with the screen locked, and the lock screen and Control Centre both carry the controls. If it stops, check that the app is allowed to play in the background and that Low Power Mode is off, since iOS suspends more aggressively under it.

Following the words is not offered for my reciter

Word-level timings are published for some reciters and not others, and the app only offers the feature where real timings exist rather than guessing at them. Choosing a different reciter in Settings will bring it back.

A surah will not load

Verses are fetched the first time you open a surah and cached afterwards, so a surah you have read before works offline but a new one needs a connection. If it persists with a good connection, the source API may be briefly down; it will load on a later attempt.

My reading streak reset

A day you read counts +1 and a day you miss counts −2, so a gap of a few days pulls the total down faster than it climbed. It never drops below zero, and reading again starts it climbing from wherever it landed.

Can I move my notes and bookmarks to a new phone?

Yes. Settings has a backup that writes a single file containing your own work — notes, bookmarks, topics, memorised surahs and reading history — which you can restore on the other device. It deliberately leaves out cached verses and downloaded audio, which the new device will fetch for itself.

Is my reading private?

Yes. What you read, bookmark, note and memorise stays on your device. The app reports which screens are used and for how long, with no identifier attached to it — the privacy page sets out exactly what is and is not recorded.

Still stuck?

Report a problem or ask for something on the issue tracker. Saying which iPhone and which iOS version you are on, and what you did just before it went wrong, makes it far quicker to fix.
